# Interaction evidence

Each pair below is anchored to a sentence that occurs verbatim in the cited source page. Quotes are copied character for character from the saved page text; each section names its source URL once.

## bitter-orange — https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/WeightLoss-HealthProfessional/

ODS topic fact sheet: Dietary Supplements for Weight Loss — Health Professional. ODS has no dedicated bitter-orange fact sheet; this sheet states the interactions below for bitter orange.

- **Cyclosporine** — "Bitter orange has also been shown to inhibit CYP3A4 activity, leading to increased blood levels of certain drugs, such as cyclosporine and saquinavir [43]."
- **Saquinavir** — "Bitter orange has also been shown to inhibit CYP3A4 activity, leading to increased blood levels of certain drugs, such as cyclosporine and saquinavir [43]."
- **Caffeine** — "Combining caffeine with other stimulants, such as bitter orange and ephedrine, can potentiate these adverse effects."

## echinacea — https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ImmuneFunction-HealthProfessional/

ODS topic fact sheet: Immune Function — Health Professional. ODS has no dedicated echinacea fact sheet; this sheet states the interactions below for echinacea.

- **Immunosuppressants** — "In addition, echinacea might reduce the effectiveness of immunosuppressants due to its potential immunostimulatory activity [224]."
- **Drugs Metabolized by Cytochrome P450** — "For example, echinacea might increase cytochrome P450 activity, thereby reducing levels of some drugs metabolized by these enzymes [223]."
