# Backlinks: what is actually achievable

Written 2026-11-16, replacing `outreach-list-top-100.md`. Every page named
below was opened and read on that date; nothing here is a guessed domain.

## The honest starting point

Google reports **one external referring domain** for herbally.app. The site is
six weeks old, has 2.4k indexed pages and 136 clicks in 28 days. At that size,
the links that actually arrive come from people who maintain link pages for a
living — librarians — not from healthline or WebMD, which do not take
unsolicited resource suggestions from unknown sites.

So the target is small and specific: **five to fifteen links in three months,
each one an editorial decision by a named librarian or practitioner.** That is
what moves a new site out of the "one referring domain" bucket.

## What there is to link to

| Asset                                    | URL                                            | Why anyone would link it                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Embeddable herb–drug interaction checker | `/embed/checker`                               | It is free, needs no account, and carries a required "Powered by HerbAlly" attribution link. Built for exactly this.                       |

The embed is the only asset _designed_ to earn links. The rest earns them by
being citable.

## Tier C — where the embed can travel

The checker is the one asset a site owner has a reason to paste in. Realistic
hosts, in order of likelihood:

1. Herbal-medicine schools and colleges with a student resources page.
2. Independent clinical herbalists with a practice site.
3. Pharmacy or nursing course pages (the same LibGuides above, second ask).
4. Natural-product retailers — **only if they sell no herb the page would flag.**
   A shop linking a checker that warns about its own products is not credible,
   and Google treats paid or reciprocal placement as a link scheme.

## What to do, in order

1. Fix nothing on our side first — the embed already works and returns 200 with
   the attribution link intact.
2. Send the Tier A email to the five guides, one at a time, from a real address,
   personalised with the one fact that applies to that guide.
3. Log each send and reply in the vault note, not in this file.
4. Re-check the same five pages in 30 days. A librarian who adds a link usually
   does it within a fortnight or not at all.

## Rules this plan follows

- No paid links, no link exchanges, no "we'll link to you if you link to us".
- No guest posts ghost-written for someone else's byline.
- No comment or forum links.
- Nothing is sent without the owner's explicit go-ahead. Drafts live here; the
  send is a human action.

## Measurement

- Google Search Console → Links, monthly. Count distinct referring domains, not
  links.
- Analytics: referrals to `/herb-drug-interactions` and `/embed/checker`.
- The number that matters is not links; it is whether the one-domain ceiling
  breaks. Five real library links would triple it.
