Round 8 completed the final two narrow B4 edge-case corrections:

   - Extended `HAZARD_WARNING_PATTERN` and `DOSE_NEGATION_PATTERN` in `src/lib/herbs/regulatory-evidence.ts` and `is_warning` / `has_negation` in `public.strip_pediatric_dosage_text` (`supabase/migrations/00100_pediatric_containment_dosage_calculations.sql`) to match `should not` / `shouldn't`, `must not` / `mustn't`, `not for use`, `not suitable`, `do not (?:give|use|take|administer)`, `never (?:give|use|take|administer)`, and French equivalents (`ne pas utiliser/prendre/administrer`, `ne doivent/doive pas`, `ne devrait/devraient pas`, `ne convient/conviennent pas`, `contre-indiqué(e)(s)`, `non recommandé(e)(s)`).
   - Scoped the adjectival `minor` token in `PEDIATRIC_KEYWORD_PATTERN` (`src/lib/herbs/regulatory-evidence.ts`) and PG16-safe `has_ped` / `v_has_ped` (`strip_pediatric_dosage_text` and `is_pediatric_only_dose_jsonb` in `00100`, using negative lookahead `\yminor\y(?!\s+(?:side\s+effects?|adverse|...)\y)` with zero lookbehind) so non-person clinical/adverse phrases such as `"Minor side effects: mild nausea if you take on an empty stomach."` are not misclassified as pediatric dosing while `"Give a minor 50 mg daily."`, `"Minors: 50 mg daily."`, and actual adult dosing (`"Adults: 500 mg twice daily."`) retain their exact expected behavior.
2. **FAQ EN/FR parity (`00100` & JS):**
   - Updated `public.herb_faqs_public`, `public.redact_herb_faqs_jsonb`, and `public.redact_faq_translations_jsonb` in `00100`, plus `sanitizeHerbPageBundle()` and `normalizePublicFaqs()` / `loadFaqsCached()` in `src/lib/herbs/herb-page-data.ts`, to drop an FAQ row **only when both** sanitized English and French answers are empty/absent.
   - When the sanitized English answer is empty/absent and the sanitized French answer is non-empty, the row is retained with English fallback question `"Safety information"` (`FAQ_FALLBACK_QUESTION_EN`), empty English answer `""`, and French sanitized question (or `"Information de sécurité"`, `FAQ_FALLBACK_QUESTION_FR`, if empty) + French answer, with zero English or French pediatric text exposure. Conversely, when the French answer is empty/absent while English is non-empty, `translations.fr` is cleared to `{ question: "", answer: "" }` so raw French pediatric questions are never leaked.
   - Bumped `unstable_cache` keys in `src/lib/herbs/herb-page-data.ts` (`herb-page-bundle-v11`, `herb-regulatory-evidence-v3`, `sources-v6-${slug}`, `monograph-v5-${slug}`, `pubmed-sheet-v5-${slug}`, `herb-faqs-v9-${slug}`).
