# HerbAlly Founder Memo

**Written:** April 11, 2026
**Updated:** June 23, 2026

---

## The Core Problem

HerbAlly has strong product potential but its credibility infrastructure
is unimplemented. The AI differentiator only works if users trust it.

An AI that gives wrong herb advice without clear limitations is worse
than no AI at all.

## What Matters (in order)

1. **Human-reviewed content.** 2,700 AI-generated monographs with no review
   is the biggest risk. Get 50-100 high-traffic herbs reviewed first.
2. **Evidence grades on pages.** A/B/C/D visible above fold, not in tabs.
3. **Source citations in AI chat.** PMID links, WHO monograph references.
4. **Prominent safety warnings.** Drug interactions above fold, not buried.
5. **"Last reviewed" dates.** On every herb page that's been reviewed.

## What Doesn't Matter Yet

- Subscription tiers, payment providers, A/B testing, email marketing.
- Three audience tiers (consumer/practitioner/herbalist). Pick one first.
- Mobile app. The PWA works.
- Writing more audit documents.

## Competitive Position

| Feature           | HerbAlly | NCCIH | Examine.com |
| ----------------- | -------- | ----- | ----------- |
| Herb count        | 2,700+   | 20    | 300+        |
| AI chat           | ✅       | ❌    | ❌          |
| Dosage calculator | ✅       | ❌    | ❌          |
| Evidence grading  | ❌       | ✅    | ✅          |
| Source citations  | Weak     | ✅    | ✅          |
| Human-reviewed    | ~0       | ✅    | ✅          |

The advantage (depth + tools + AI) is real. The gap (trust + evidence)
is what kills it. Close the gap.
