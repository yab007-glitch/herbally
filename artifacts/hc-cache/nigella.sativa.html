NIGELLA OIL - NIGELLA SATIVA

(PDF Version - 62 KB)

This monograph is intended to serve as a guide to industry for the preparation of Product Licence Applications (PLAs) and labels for natural health product market authorization. It is not intended to be a comprehensive review of the medicinal ingredient.

Notes

  • Text in parentheses is additional optional information which can be included on the label at the applicant's discretion.
  • The solidus (/) indicates that either term and/or statement may be selected on the label.
  • This monograph supports the fixed oil of Nigella sativa seed which is the non-volatile lipid fraction obtained from the seeds. It consists mainly of triglycerides (fatty acids) such as linoleic acid, oleic acid, and palmitic acid, along with minor components like sterols, tocopherols, and phenolic compounds. Essential/volatile oil preparations (e.g., those prepared by distillation) are not within the scope of this monograph.

Route of administration

Oral

Dosage form(s)

This monograph excludes foods or food-like dosage forms as indicated in the Natural Health Products Management of Applications Policy.

Acceptable dosage forms for oral use are indicated in the dosage form drop-down list of the web-based Product Licence Application form for Compendial applications.

Use(s) or Purpose(s)

  • Source of antioxidants/Provides antioxidants (Tavakoli-Rouzbehani et al. 2022; Namazi et al. 2015; Singh et al. 2014).
  • Source of antioxidants/Provides antioxidants that help fight/protect (cell) against/reduce (the oxidative effect of/the oxidative damage caused by/cell damage caused by) free radicals (Tavakoli-Rouzbehani et al. 2022; Namazi et al. 2015; Singh et al. 2014).
  • Helps support liver function (Khonche et al. 2019; Rashidmayvan et al. 2019).
  • Helps support/maintain normal low density lipoprotein (LDL) cholesterol levels (Kooshki et al. 2020; Rashidmayvan et al. 2019).
  • Helps support/maintain normal (blood) triglyceride/triacylglycerol levels (Rashidmayvan et al. 2019).
  • Helps support/maintain (normal) heart/cardiovascular health (Kooshki et al. 2020; Huseini et al. 2013).
  • Helps support/maintain healthy blood pressure levels (Shoaei-Hagh et al. 2021; Huseini et al. 2013).
  • Source of linoleic acid (LA) for the maintenance of good health (HC 2025).
  • Source of an omega-6 fatty acid for the maintenance of good health (HC 2025).
  • Source of an essential fatty acid for the maintenance of good health (HC 2025).
  • Source of oleic acid (OA) (HC 2025).

Notes

  • The recommended uses can be combined on the product label (e.g., Helps support liver function and cardiovascular health).
  • The terms ‘Helps’ or ‘Helps to’ can be used interchangeably on the label.

Dose(s)

Subpopulation(s)

Adults 18 years and older

Quantity(ies)

Method of preparation: Oil fixed; Oil fixed standardized

Antioxidants

Up to 5 grams of seed oil per day; Not to exceed 2.5 grams per single dose (Shoaei-Hagh et al. 2021; Khonche et al. 2019; Hosseini et al. 2013; Huseini et al. 2013).

Liver function; Cholesterol levels; Triglyceride levels; Cardiovascular health

1 - 5 grams of seed oil per day; Not to exceed 2.5 grams per single dose (Kooshki et al. 2020; Khonche et al. 2019; Rashidmayvan et al. 2019; Huseini et al. 2013).

Healthy blood pressure

4.6 grams (5 mL) - 5 grams (5.4 mL) of seed oil per day; Not to exceed 2.5 grams (2.7 mL) per single dose (Mohtashami et al. 2011; Shoaei-Hagh et al. 2021; Huseini et al. 2013).

Method of preparation: Oil fixed standardized

Source of linoleic acid; Source of an omega-6 fatty acid; Source of an essential fatty acid

Up to 5 grams of seed oil per day; Not to exceed 2.5 grams per single dose, providing at least 850 milligrams linoleic acid per day (HC 2025; Shoaei-Hagh et al. 2021; Khonche et al. 2019; Hosseini et al. 2013; Huseini et al. 2013).

Source of oleic acid

Up to 5 grams of seed oil per day; Not to exceed 2.5 grams per single dose, providing at least 100 milligrams oleic acid per day (HC 2025; Shoaei-Hagh et al. 2021; Khonche et al. 2019; Hosseini et al. 2013; Huseini et al. 2013).

All products

Notes

  • The amount of the Nigella fixed oil may also be represented as a volumetric quantity (i.e., mL). Reported densities for Nigella seed oil vary in the literature; using an average density of approximately 0.92 g/mL, 5 g corresponds to approximately 5.43 mL and 5 mL corresponds to approximately 4.6 g.
  • Optional potency information: Thymoquinone may also be declared. Thymoquinone content in Nigella fixed oils may vary depending on the source material and manufacturing conditions. Conventional cold-pressed fixed oils generally contain lower thymoquinone concentrations, whereas higher concentrations have also been reported in fixed oils evaluated in clinical studies reviewed for this monograph. This information is provided for ingredient characterization purposes only and is not intended as a safety limit.

Direction(s) for use

Optional

Take with/after a meal/food (Tavakoli-Rouzbehani et al. 2021; Hosseini et al. 2013; Huseini et al. 2013).

Duration(s) of use

All products

Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician for use beyond 12 weeks (Khonche et al. 2019; Hosseini et al. 2013).

Cholesterol levels; Triglyceride levels; Healthy blood pressure

Use for at least 8 weeks to see beneficial effects (Shoaei-Hagh et al. 2021; Kooshki et al. 2020; Rashidmayvan et al. 2019).

Risk information

Caution(s) and warning(s)

  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you have an immune or blood pressure disorder, or diabetes (Ciesielska-Figlon et al. 2023; Salem et al. 2023; Moustafa et al. 2019; Huseini et al. 2013).
  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you are breastfeeding.

Contraindication(s)

Do not use if you are pregnant (Mohammadi et al. 2024).

Known adverse reaction(s)

  • When using this product you may experience gastrointestinal discomfort/disturbances (Tavakoli-Rouzbehani et al. 2021; Hosseini et al. 2013; Huseini et al. 2013).
  • Stop use if hypersensitivity/allergy occurs (Ali and Blunden 2003; Al-Sheikh and Gad el-Rab 1996).

Non-medicinal ingredients

Must be chosen from the current Natural Health Products Ingredients Database (NHPID) and must meet the limitations outlined in the database.

Storage conditions

Must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural Health Products Regulations.

Specifications

  • The finished product specifications must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural and Non-prescription Health Products Directorate (NNHPD) Quality of Natural Health Products Guide.
  • The medicinal ingredient must comply with the requirements outlined in the NHPID.
