TONGKAT ALI - EURYCOMA LONGIFOLIA

(PDF Version - 62 KB)

This monograph is intended to serve as a guide to industry for the preparation of Product Licence Applications (PLAs) and labels for natural health product market authorization. It is not intended to be a comprehensive review of the medicinal ingredient.

Notes

  • Text in parentheses is additional optional information which can be included on the label at the applicant's discretion.
  • The solidus (/) indicates that either term and/or statement may be selected on the label.

Date

October 30, 2026

Proper name(s), Common name(s), Source information

Table 1. Proper name(s), Common name(s), Source information
Proper name(s) Common name(s) Source information
Source material(s) Part(s) Preparation(s)
Eurycoma longifolia Tongkat ali Eurycoma longifolia Root Dry

Reference: NHPID 2026.

Route of administration

Oral

Dosage form(s)

This monograph excludes foods or food-like dosage forms as indicated in the Natural Health Products Management of Applications Policy.

Acceptable dosage forms for oral use are indicated in the dosage form drop-down list of the web-based Product Licence Application form for Compendial applications.

Use(s) or Purpose(s)

  • Helps support (men's) healthy sexual interest (Ismail et al. 2012).
  • Helps promote testosterone production (in men) (Leisegang et al. 2022; Chinnappan et al. 2021; Talbott et al. 2013; Henkel et al. 2014; Tambi et al. 2012).
  • Supports testosterone production (in men) (Leisegang et al. 2022; Chinnappan et al. 2021; Talbott et al. 2013; Henkel et al. 2014; Tambi et al. 2012).

Notes

  • The above uses can be combined on the product label (e.g., Helps support healthy sexual interest and promote testosterone production in men).
  • The terms ‘Helps’ or ‘Helps to’ can be used interchangeably on the label.

Dose(s)

Subpopulation(s)

Adult males 18 years and older

Quantity(ies)

Methods of preparation: Standardized Aqueous Extracts (Dry extract)

Sexual interest

300 - 400 milligrams of extract per day, standardized to 0.8 - 1.5% eurycomanone.

Optional: 20 - 22% eurypeptides, 22 - 45% total protein, 40 - 65% glycosaponins (total saponins), and/or 28 - 55% total polysaccharides (Le et al. 2025; Abd Aziz et al. 2021; EFSA 2021; Chen et al. 2014; Henkel et al. 2014; Ismail et al. 2012; Malaysian Standards 2011).

Testosterone production

200 - 400 milligrams of extract per day, standardized to 0.8 - 1.5% eurycomanone.

Optional: 20 - 22% eurypeptides, 22 - 45% total protein, 40 - 65% glycosaponins (total saponins), and/or 28 - 55% total polysaccharides (Le et al. 2025; Abd Aziz et al. 2021; Chinnappan et al. 2021; EFSA 2021; Leitão et al. 2021; Chen et al. 2014; Henkel et al. 2014; Talbott et al. 2013; Malaysian Standards 2011).

Notes

  • Eurypeptides is a term used specifically to describe peptide fractions of approximately 4.2-4.3 kDa associated with patented extraction and fractionation processes.
  • Glycosaponins and total saponins are used interchangeably in the Eurycoma longifolia literature and may be used interchangeably in specifications and labelling. As only the glycosaponins fraction is water soluble, representing it as either total saponins or glycosaponins is accurate for an aqueous extract. Therefore, the term "glycosaponins" may be replaced with "total saponins" on the product label.
  • Eurycomanone must be declared on the PLA form and product label. Eurypeptides, total protein, glycosaponins, and total polysaccharides may be declared where applicable.

Direction(s) for use

No statement required.

Duration(s) of use

Products providing more than 200 milligrams and up to 300 milligrams of standardized extract, per day

Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician for use beyond 12 weeks (Chinnappan et al. 2021; Udani et al. 2014; Ismail et al. 2012).

Products providing more than 300 milligrams of standardized extract, per day

Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician for use beyond 6 weeks (Chen et al. 2014).

Risk information

Caution(s) and warning(s)

All products

  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you are taking propranolol (Salman et al. 2010).
  • Stop use and ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician if you experience any new symptoms including yellowing of the eyes or skin, dark urine, nausea, vomiting, stomach pain (NIH 2024).

Sexual interest

  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you have been diagnosed with low sexual desire or erectile dysfunction.
  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you have a psychological disorder such as anxiety or depression (Brotto 2010).

Contraindication(s)

  • Do not use if you have a liver disorder (NIH 2024).
  • Do not use if you have benign prostate hypertrophy or prostate cancer (EFSA 2021; Henkel et al. 2014; Talbott et al. 2013; Tambi et al. 2012).

Known adverse reaction(s)

No statement required.

Non-medicinal ingredients

Must be chosen from the current Natural Health Products Ingredients Database (NHPID) and must meet the limitations outlined in the database.

Storage conditions

Must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural Health Products Regulations.

Specifications

  • The finished product specifications must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural and Non-prescription Health Products Directorate (NNHPD) Quality of Natural Health Products Guide.
  • The medicinal ingredient must comply with the requirements outlined in the NHPID.
